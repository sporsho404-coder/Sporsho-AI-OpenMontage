## How To Use This Table

**Outcome** values:

- `shipped-clean` — approved with no revision rounds
- `shipped-n-revisions` — approved after N client rounds
- `abandoned` — did not ship; **always record why**
- `in-progress`

The **Lesson?** column points to the `LESSONS.md` anchor, or `—` if nothing new was learned.

## Why `abandoned` Rows Matter

The most expensive knowledge is the knowledge that stops you repeating a failure.
An abandoned project with a clear reason may be worth more than ten clean shipments.

## Pruning

Rows here are cheap — a table row is a few hundred bytes. Keep them all. This is the
longitudinal record that shows which pipelines and registers actually work for your
clients over time.
